## Entry

> **Velcro**

6 characters. Exactly one word — the word my ex would use to describe me.

She said it like a pet name. It was not a pet name. Velcro sticks to
everything, follows you room to room, and peels off loud. That is the
whole relationship, in one word: affectionate on the surface, a roast
underneath — funny because it is honest, and it stings a little because
it is true.
